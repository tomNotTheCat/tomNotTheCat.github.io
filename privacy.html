Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This is a personal rclone setup used to access my own Google Drive account. It is not a public service.

rclone accesses Google Drive data according to the permissions I grant through Google's OAuth authorization. Google processes requests as part of providing Google Drive.

OAuth credentials are stored in rclone's local configuration on my computer. I do not operate a separate server that collects Google Drive data from other users.

I do not sell Google Drive data or use it for advertising. Access can be revoked through my Google Account security settings.
